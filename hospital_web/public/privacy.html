Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Introduction

LifeLink ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web portal.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Name, email, phone number, blood group, city, location (if permitted).
  • Health Data: Blood donation history, medical facility information (for hospitals).
  • Usage Data: App usage, feature interactions, device information.
  • Cookies & Tracking: We use cookies to enhance user experience and analyze traffic.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To match blood donors with hospitals in emergencies
  • To send emergency alerts and notifications
  • To improve our platform and personalize user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Hospitals and blood banks for emergency matching (limited to necessary data)
  • Service providers hosting our platform
  • Legal authorities if required by law
  • Business transfers (in case of merger or acquisition)

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS, AES-256) and follow best practices to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable)
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@lifelink.example.

International Data Transfers

If you are located outside India, your information may be transferred to and processed in India. By using our services, you consent to such transfers.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
